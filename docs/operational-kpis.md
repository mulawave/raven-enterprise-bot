# Operational KPIs & Ownership

Date: 2026-01-30

## KPI Definitions, Targets, and Owners

- Uptime
  - Definition: Percentage of time the production API is reachable and responding successfully.
  - Target: ≥ 99.9% monthly
  - Owner: Operations Lead

- Order Success Rate
  - Definition: Successful order creation and completion divided by total order attempts.
  - Target: ≥ 98% weekly
  - Owner: Ordering Lead

- Booking Success Rate
  - Definition: Successful booking creation and confirmation divided by total booking attempts.
  - Target: ≥ 98% weekly
  - Owner: Booking Lead

- Payment Success Rate
  - Definition: Successful payment verifications divided by total payment attempts.
  - Target: ≥ 97% weekly
  - Owner: Payments Lead

- AI Fallback Rate
  - Definition: AI fallback responses divided by total AI interactions.
  - Target: ≤ 10% weekly
  - Owner: AI Lead

- Support Response Time
  - Definition: Median time to first response for support tickets.
  - Target: ≤ 4 hours
  - Owner: Support Lead
