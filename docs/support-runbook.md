# Support Escalation Runbook

Date: 2026-01-30

## Severity Levels
- Sev 1: Full outage or major data integrity risk
- Sev 2: Partial outage or severe degradation
- Sev 3: Limited impact or localized issue
- Sev 4: Minor issues or informational requests

## Response Times
- Sev 1: Acknowledge within 15 minutes, update every 30 minutes
- Sev 2: Acknowledge within 30 minutes, update every 2 hours
- Sev 3: Acknowledge within 4 hours, update daily
- Sev 4: Acknowledge within 1 business day, update as needed

## Escalation Paths
- Sev 1: Support Lead → Operations Lead → Engineering Lead
- Sev 2: Support Lead → Operations Lead
- Sev 3: Support Lead → On-call Engineer
- Sev 4: Support Lead

## Communication Templates (Text Only)
- Acknowledgement: "We have received your report and are investigating. We will provide updates according to severity timelines."
- Update: "We are actively working on the issue. Current status: [status]. Next update by: [time]."
- Resolution: "The issue is resolved. Summary: [summary]. If you observe any remaining impact, please let us know."
- Post-Incident: "We have completed a review and will share a summary of findings and follow-up actions."
